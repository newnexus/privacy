Delete Your Account & Data

Guess When · Last updated 3 August 2026

You can ask us to permanently delete your Guess When profile and everything stored with it, at any time — even if you have already uninstalled the app.

How to request deletion

Email us with your in-game tag and we will delete your account and data:

Email a deletion request

Or email kozzzygames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account and data” and include your tag.

Where to find your tag: open the app, go to Settings → About — your tag is shown there. We need it because Guess When accounts are anonymous (no email or username), so the tag is the only way for us to find your data.

What gets deleted

DataRemoved
Your public nickname / tagYes
Your friends list and friend linksYes
Your leaderboard scoresYes
Any card reports you submittedYes
Your anonymous account recordYes

How long it takes

Requests are processed within 30 days. Once deleted, your profile and the data listed above are removed and cannot be recovered.

Questions about your data? Email kozzzygames@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
