Privacy Policy — Guess When

Last updated 1 August 2026

Guess When is a history trivia game published by KozzzyGames (package com.kozzzygames.guesswhen). This policy explains what the game stores, what it sends off your device, and who else can see it.

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location, and it has no account to sign up for. You are identified only by a randomly generated ID and by a nickname you choose yourself.

One thing worth knowing about the nickname. When the game first asks what to call you, it pre-fills the box with your device's name — because that is usually a better guess than "Player". Some devices are named after their owner. Nothing is sent anywhere until you confirm, and you can type anything you like over it, but if you accept the suggestion then that is the name other players will see. You can change it later from your Profile.

What is sent to our servers

The game uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database and Firestore) as its backend. The following is stored there:

An anonymous user ID automatically generated by Firebase AuthenticationTo tell one player from another. It is not linked to a Google account, an email address or any identity you hold.
Your nickname and player tag (for example Anna#4821)So friends can find you and opponents can see who they are playing
Your best scoreThe public leaderboard
Friend connections and invitationsThe friends list
Temporary multiplayer data — a queue entry while you look for an opponent, and the match itselfTo pair you with another player and run the game. Removed when you stop searching or leave.

Google may additionally process technical information such as your IP address and device type in the course of operating these services. See Firebase's privacy information.

Pictures on the cards

The photographs and artwork shown on question cards are not stored by us. They are freely licensed images downloaded directly from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) the first time a card appears, and then kept on your device so they are available offline and are never fetched twice. This means your device makes a request to Wikimedia, and the Wikimedia Foundation may log it in the ordinary course of serving the file. See the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy. Every image is credited, with its licence, under Settings → Image credits.

What other players can see

The following are public to other players of the game:

  • Your nickname and player tag — these are searchable, so anyone can look you up by tag in order to add you as a friend.
  • Your best score and position on the leaderboard.

Your anonymous user ID is never shown to other players. Please do not use your real name, or any information you would not want to be public, as your nickname.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video adverts, supplied by Google AdMob. They are never forced on you: an advert only plays if you choose to watch one to regain a used hint or lifeline. There are no banner or interstitial adverts.

Google AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier and related technical data in order to serve and measure adverts. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised advertising data is used, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the game. If you decline, the game continues to work exactly as before; you will simply not be offered adverts.

Analytics

The Firebase software the game uses includes Google Analytics for Firebase. The game does not send any custom analytics events of its own, but this component may automatically collect general usage information such as app opens, session length, device model, operating system version and country. It is not used to identify you personally.

Children

Guess When is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, please contact us and we will remove it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data is kept for as long as you use the game. Temporary multiplayer data is deleted automatically when a search or match ends.

To have your stored data removed — your nickname, tag, leaderboard entry and friend connections — email the address below. Please include your player tag so the right record can be found. Uninstalling the game removes everything held on your device, but does not by itself remove your leaderboard entry.

Your rights

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, use the contact address below. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
